After refactoring my native macOS port of Call to Power 2, I finally sat down for my first long campaign. I did not have time for one long sitting, but over three sessions I reached the modern era. My latest regular save was at turn 615, in AD 2015.

I saved and loaded the game between sessions. Loading is a little slower than in the retail version at the moment.

The success was simple: **the game did not crash during those sessions.**

Passing tests matters, but this felt different. At first, every click on “Next Turn” came with the question: will it hang this time? As the campaign went on, I gradually stopped expecting something to break. The next turn arrived, and I kept playing.

It felt like it used to, years ago. I was thinking about the game rather than the code behind it.

![Call to Power 2 in AD 2016, showing a developed empire, coastal cities, and futuristic units.](/img/posts/ctp2-615-turns/gameplay.png)

*The same campaign in AD 2016, one turn beyond the regular save described here. Screenshot taken on November 18.*

There are still sprite problems that I have not resolved. This was not proof that every bug was gone. But those visual issues did not stop me from enjoying the campaign, and nothing crashed along the way.

Is the gameplay the same as in the original game? It felt the same to me, and I hope it is. But it needs more testing, especially the computer players' AI.

For the first time, I felt we were getting close. I was simply playing the game again.
